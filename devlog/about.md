Charoite is a fully local AI meeting assistant for macOS: it records the
microphone and system audio, transcribes and diarizes on device, answers
questions mid-meeting, and afterwards builds an Obsidian knowledge graph that
remembers people, systems and decisions across meetings. No cloud by default,
no accounts, no telemetry. Apache-2.0, Apple Silicon.

This devlog is the working side of it — what got measured, what broke, and
which decisions were reversed after looking at real numbers. Posts land here
first; the code is in
[charoiteai/Charoite_audio](https://github.com/charoiteai/Charoite_audio).
